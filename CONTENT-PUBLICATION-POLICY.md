# Public Content and Catalog Publication Policy

## Rule

Raw, AI-extracted, historical, or owner-unreviewed data is never public by default.

Legal and patient-facing notices follow the same boundary. See `docs/legal/README.md` and
`docs/legal/approval-ledger.md`; all eight legal document records are draft-only until owner,
clinical, and qualified legal/privacy approvals are recorded.

## Required approval for every test/package

- [ ] Canonical public name
- [ ] Included parameters/tests
- [ ] Specimen and collection container
- [ ] Patient preparation/fasting
- [ ] Reference/report wording reviewed
- [ ] Current customer price
- [ ] Crossed-out/list value justified by included tests
- [ ] Turnaround estimate
- [ ] Home collection availability
- [ ] Category and search aliases
- [ ] Legal/clinical wording reviewed
- [ ] Owner approval recorded

## Source classes

### Raw inventory
Historical registers and AI-extracted lists. Useful for discovery only. Never publish directly.

### Draft record
Normalized record awaiting owner review. May be used in internal review tools only.

### Approved record
Owner-reviewed record allowed in the public catalog and static SEO pages.

## Claims policy

Do not publish unsupported accreditation, accuracy, certification, encryption, turnaround, review, or availability claims. If a fact changes over time, store it in configuration or approved content—not scattered UI literals.

## Revisions

Every price or parameter change should preserve the prior approved record in version control or an external content audit trail. Do not silently rewrite historical patient reports.
