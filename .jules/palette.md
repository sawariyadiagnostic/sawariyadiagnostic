## 2024-05-19 - Improved Search and Selection Empty States
**Learning:** Empty states without clear visual cues or next actions lead to dead ends; adding simple icons and fallback actions (like a demo ID) drastically improves the perceived utility of search interfaces.
**Action:** When implementing search or list components in this app, always design empty states with an icon, descriptive helper text, and a low-friction "try it out" action button (e.g. using demo data).
