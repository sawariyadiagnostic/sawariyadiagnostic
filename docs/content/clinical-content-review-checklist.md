# Clinical content review checklist

This checklist is a release gate, not a substitute for clinical or legal advice.

## Record identity

- [ ] Canonical test/package name confirmed
- [ ] Aliases and duplicate records checked
- [ ] Source register/report template identified
- [ ] Publication state is explicitly approved

## Clinical content

- [ ] Purpose and scope are accurate
- [ ] Preparation instructions are approved
- [ ] Specimen and collection requirements are approved
- [ ] Timing/fasting/medication cautions are approved
- [ ] Reference ranges are not invented or presented without the correct method/context
- [ ] Interpretation language does not diagnose or promise an outcome
- [ ] Limitations and clinician follow-up guidance are appropriate
- [ ] Units, spelling, abbreviations, and Hindi translation are reviewed

## Commercial content

- [ ] Customer price approved
- [ ] Listed/value price supported by the approved calculation/source
- [ ] Availability and service area confirmed
- [ ] Turnaround is stated only if operationally confirmed
- [ ] No insurer-branded package or unapproved claim is included

## Technical publication

- [ ] Stable slug and title
- [ ] Accessible heading structure
- [ ] No patient data or secrets
- [ ] JSON-LD matches visible content
- [ ] Canonical/hreflang links are correct
- [ ] Content validation and build pass
